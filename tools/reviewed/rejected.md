# Reviewed and left out

Database exercises that were reviewed but not added, with the reason. Database name — reason.

- Floor Press — near-duplicate of dumbbell floor press; rack setup unclear in the photos.
- Reverse Grip Bent-Over Rows — start and finish photos show the same position.
- Dumbbell Lying Rear Lateral Raise — same chest-supported setup as the incline-bench reverse fly.
- Butt Lift (Bridge) — both photos show nearly the same position.
- Donkey Calf Raises — needs a partner on your back.
- Band Hip Adductions — no reference page for this setup.
- Tricep Dumbbell Kickback — StrengthLog calls it a poor choice (little resistance through most of the range).
- Overhead Cable Curl — the photos show a two-cable version; StrengthLog describes a single high pulley.
- Lateral Raise - With Bands — standing on the band isn't the version StrengthLog or ACE describe.
- Cable Seated Lateral Raise — replaced by the one-arm standing version the reference describes.
- Seated Dumbbell Press — StrengthLog's version uses a raised backrest; this one doesn't.
- Dips - Triceps Version — StrengthLog describes one bar dip; kept the chest version as "Bar dip".
- V-Bar Pullup — hooking a V-handle over the pull-up bar isn't covered by any reference; use the neutral-grip pull-up on fixed handles.
- Triceps Overhead Extension with Rope — duplicate of the overhead cable triceps extension already in the app.
- Kettlebell Seated Press — the photos show it seated on the floor after a clean; StrengthLog's version is seated on a bench with the bell already in the rack position.
- Extended Range One-Arm Kettlebell Floor Press — the crossed-leg setup isn't covered by any reference.
- Kettlebell Arnold Press — starts with a clean (explosive lifts are left out) and no reference covers a kettlebell Arnold press.
- Alternating Kettlebell Row — near-duplicate of the one- and two-arm kettlebell rows.
- Alternating Kettlebell Press — starts with a clean, and near-duplicate of the two-arm kettlebell press.
- Seated Dumbbell Palms-Up Wrist Curl — near-duplicate of the one-arm seated version (which matches the reference).
- Seated Dumbbell Palms-Down Wrist Curl — near-duplicate of the one-arm seated version (which matches the reference).
- Cable Chest Press — removed after a second look: its photos show a seated cable press; StrengthLog's cable chest press is standing with the pulleys high, and no reference covers the seated version.
- Standing Cable Chest Press — the photos use chest-height pulleys; StrengthLog's version uses the top pulleys and a forward lean.
- High Cable Curls — the photos (lying on a bench under a high pulley) don't match its own instructions (standing between two pulleys); the lying version is added separately.
- Calf Press — near-duplicate of the leg press calf raise.
- Hanging Pike — no reference for the inverted pike to the bar.
- Chair Squat — StrengthLog's chair squat is bodyweight to a chair; the photos show a barbell squat without a chair.
- Alternating Renegade Row — the photos show a bent-over stance, not the push-up position the reference describes.
